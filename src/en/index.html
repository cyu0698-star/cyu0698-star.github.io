About

Hi! I'm Yuqian — an AI product designer at DeepChatBI, a Silicon Valley AI company, whose work spans the entire product chain as a UX / interaction designer.

I led the complete 0-to-1 cycle of ChatBI, a conversational BI SaaS — from user research and product-flow planning (including the four-stage agent journey "data ingestion → insight analysis → smart action → auto execution") to interaction and visual design, testing, and pre-launch design QA. In the AI era, my core strength beyond a pure UI/UX designer is independently driving a product end to end and owning the outcome.

In 2026 I brought this to hardware-plus-software products at Flowtica (Shenzhen, AI recorder and e-ink devices): the main flow and every edge case of the "Help-You-Organize" AI feature on Web / Mac, the iOS flows of the Verso e-ink business card, and keeping iOS / Mac / Android / Web in sync.

BA in Digital Product Design, HBK Essen (09/2020 – 04/2025), focused on hardware-plus-software smart devices and consumer electronics; admitted to the MA Service Design at Loughborough University (09/2025) — and chose to go all-in on AI.
