30-sec overview

A software flow under hardware constraints: the e-ink screen is greyscale only and refreshes slowly, so the app has to make the card’s “edit → publish to the device → the other person sends details back” crystal clear.

My path: first break the PRD down into a frame-level flow list, then build two galleries from real captures (card interactions, competitors) to lay out the decision points, and finally deliver 47 frames of iOS flows: entry (A1–A4), card home and the Update flow (B0–B13, including draft autosave, required-field validation, link validation, offline, updating / success / failure) and wallpaper preview (W9).

01 What the product is

An e-ink screen that shows your business card, and the app behind it

On the device: the e-ink screen shows the card face; an NFC tap or a QR scan takes the other person to a web card, a custom link or “save as contact”. In the app (PRD V2.0): device settings → Contact Card, where you set name, title, contact details, Opens (what opens after a tap) and the Bio page; later PRDs added generating a greyscale card face from a photo of a business card, uploading a logo, and three templates.

Competitor reference: digital business cards such as Blinq, HiHello, Popl, Mobilo and Linq rely on phone-to-phone taps or QR codes and have no screen of their own. Verso’s difference is that “the card itself is the screen” — so what the card face looks like, when it refreshes and what happens offline all become design questions.

03 Flows & states

Card home and the Update flow

The home screen is an editable card + an Update button at the bottom: any change saves a draft automatically and enables Update; Update publishes the card to the device, with three states in between — updating, success, failure; offline you can edit but not Update.

04 Hardware constraints & open questions

An e-ink screen is not a phone screen

  • Greyscale and resolution: the card face is greyscale only, so photos and logos have to be converted to greyscale before they go on screen; the exact resolution and number of grey levels was the first open question I raised with the PM.
  • Refresh: e-ink refreshes slowly and leaves ghosting, so Update needs clear “updating / success / failure” feedback, instead of the instant effect people expect from phone apps.
  • Content length: the Bio character count, which of several contact entries goes on screen, and the final copy weren’t covered by the PRD; the delivery version uses placeholders + open-question notes.
Open questions (for the PM): screen resolution and grey levels · Bio character limit · rules for which of several contact entries is used · final copy · how to flag it when, offline, the draft and the content on the device don’t match.

05 Next · redesign (in progress)

Page hierarchy and UI polish

The delivery version’s goal was “draw every state the PRD defines, so engineering can build it”. Next, as a personal retrospective, I’m redoing two things: ① page hierarchy — re-layering entry, card home, Contact Card, Customize and device settings to cut down on jumping back and forth; ② UI polish — making the card-face preview and the card home visually consistent, in the style of the Verso page on Flowtica’s official website. The demo will use content from two PRDs: photo card creation and logo templates.
